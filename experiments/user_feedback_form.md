- Give it to classmates or adults after they use the app for about 5 minutes.
- Do not collect names, addresses, or health information. Number the forms instead.
- Record the answers in `experiments/results/user_feedback_results.csv` with these columns:

- Results: `[RESULT: number of testers]`, `[RESULT: average for question 1]`,
